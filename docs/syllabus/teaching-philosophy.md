# Teaching Approach

The primary objective of this course is to equip students with a practical and durable framework for analyzing business as an intergral part of our society, its challenges and opportunities.

My teaching methodology is centered on application. The course is structured as a hands-on workshop where learning is achieved by applying core business concepts to real-world situations. The focus is on developing analytical and problem-solving skills through project-based work, rather than on the memorization of abstract theories.

The curriculum is designed to demonstrate the interconnectedness of key business functions. Students will learn to analyze how decisions in areas like finance, marketing, and operations influence one another and impact overall strategy.

Upon completion, students are expected to be able to:

*   Analyze a company’s strategic decisions and performance using evidence-based reasoning.
*   Explain the relationships between the primary functions of a business.
*   Construct and defend a persuasive, data-driven business analysis.
*   Critically evaluate different management ideologies and their consequences.

Students are encouraged to use modern professional tools, including AI assistants, responsibly and ethically. The goal is to build proficiency with the technologies used in the contemporary workplace while emphasizing the importance of individual critical thinking and analytical ownership.
