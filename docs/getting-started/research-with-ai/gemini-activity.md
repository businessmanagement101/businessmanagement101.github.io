# Gemini: Your AI Collaborator

**Hands-On Activity**

Now that you've used NotebookLM to extract and understand the key facts from your source documents, it's time to think about what those facts *mean*. For this, we'll turn to Gemini, your creative and reasoning partner. We will use the insights you just gathered as the factual foundation for both strategic analysis and deeper conceptual understanding, which are central to your main course projects.

**Objective:** To use Gemini as a partner to brainstorm strategic ideas for your Company Analysis Project and to explore theoretical connections for your Concept Map Project.


## :material-numeric-1-circle: Synthesize Your Findings

Before moving to Gemini, you must first distill your research from NotebookLM into a concise summary. This is a critical step where you, the human analyst, identify the most important takeaways.

1.  Review your **"Ford Company Analysis"** notebook in NotebookLM. Look at your saved notes, the chat history, and the generated summaries.
2.  In a new note or a separate document, write a short paragraph (3-4 sentences) that summarizes the most crucial information and key tensions you discovered.

**Example Synthesis:**
> "My analysis shows that Ford is aggressively pivoting to electric vehicles, particularly by electrifying its popular truck lines. However, the company's own 10-K report identifies significant supply chain risks for batteries and semiconductors. While the leadership team expresses confidence in recent earnings calls, there is a clear conflict between their ambitious production goals and these external dependencies."

## :material-numeric-2-circle: Applying Your Research with Gemini

Your summary is now the high-quality "context" you will provide to Gemini. This ensures that its creative and conceptual brainstorming is grounded in your actual research. Below are two distinct ways to use this context, each aligned with a major course project.

### :material-alpha-a-circle-outline: Company Analysis Project 
**Strategic Thinking**

For your company analysis, you need to move beyond just stating facts to providing insightful analysis and recommendations. Gemini is the perfect partner for this kind of strategic brainstorming.

**Prompts to try:**

* **For Strategic Planning:**

    > "Based on the summary above, act as a business strategy consultant. What are three potential growth opportunities for Ford outside of North America that would leverage their EV strengths but minimize their stated supply chain risks?"

* **For Problem-Solving:**

    > "Given the risks I've identified in my summary, draft a short, persuasive memo to a senior executive. The memo should outline a three-point plan to mitigate the biggest supply chain risk for EV batteries."

### :material-alpha-b-circle-outline:  Concept Map Project 

**Connecting Theory to Practice**

Your concept map project requires you to organize theoretical concepts and use them to analyze a situation. You can use Gemini to deepen your understanding of these concepts and see how they apply to a real life example.

**Prompts to try:**

* **To Learn a New Concept:**

    > "My research summary on Ford is above. The 'supply chain risk' I identified seems related to the business concept of **'Vertical Integration.'** Can you explain vertical integration to me in simple terms, using an analogy? Then, explain how it specifically relates to Ford's EV battery situation."

* **To Relate Different Concepts:**

    > "Based on my summary, I see a tension between the concepts of **'Brand Loyalty'** and **'Disruptive Innovation.'** Act as a business school professor and explain the relationship between these two concepts. Use Ford's situation with its established brand versus new EV startups as the primary case to illustrate your points."

* **To Generate a Narrative for Your Map:**

    > "I am creating a concept map about **'Competitive Advantage.'** Based on my research summary on Ford, draft a short narrative paragraph that explains how 'Manufacturing Scale' and 'Distribution Networks' act as competitive advantages for Ford, but how 'Technological Agility' might be a weakness. This text will help me connect the concepts on my map."
