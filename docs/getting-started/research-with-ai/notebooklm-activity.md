# Company Research with NotebookLM
**Hands-On Activity** 

This activity is designed to move you from theory to practice. You'll use NotebookLM to analyze Ford Motor Company, transforming a collection of documents into a dynamic and queryable knowledge base.

**Objective:** To learn the core workflow of NotebookLM by exploring single sources, synthesizing information across multiple sources, and generating structured summaries.

## :material-numeric-1-circle: Gather Your Sources

To begin, download the curated set of files your instructor has provided. This collection includes Ford's latest Form 10-K, proxy filings, and a Q4 earnings call transcript. You'll also find a text file containing links to a YouTube interview and a Google Doc. This variety is intentional—it highlights NotebookLM's ability to handle diverse source formats, including PDFs, TXT files, and even content from Google Drive and YouTube links.


## :material-numeric-2-circle: Create Your Notebook & Explore the Interface

Now, let's set up your workspace.

1.  Navigate to NotebookLM and create a new notebook named **"Ford Company Analysis."**
2.  Upload all the files from Step 1 into your new notebook.
3.  Take a moment to get familiar with the interface, which is organized into three main panels:
    * **Sources Panel (Left):** This is your library. All the documents you've uploaded are listed here. You can click on any source to view it.
    * **Chat Panel (Center):** This is your main interaction space. Here, you'll ask questions and receive AI-generated, source-grounded answers.
    * **Studio (Right):** This is your project workspace. Think of it as a smart notebook where you can save key insights from the chat, write your own notes, and generate high-level summaries of your sources.




## :material-numeric-3-circle: Dive into a Single Source

Before asking questions across all documents, let's explore just one.

1.  In the **Sources** panel, click on the **Form 10-K** document.
2.  The document will open, and alongside it, NotebookLM will automatically generate a **Source Guide**. This guide includes a summary, key topics, and even suggested questions.
3.  Click on one of the **Key Topics** (e.g., "Risk Factors" or "Business Segments").
4.  Observe how clicking the topic automatically populates the **Chat** panel with a question and generates a detailed, cited answer. This is a powerful way to get a quick overview of a long document.


## :material-numeric-4-circle: Ask Questions Across ***ALL*** Sources

The true power of NotebookLM is its ability to synthesize information from *all* your sources at once. Use the chat feature to ask broader questions that require connecting ideas from different documents.

**Try these prompts:**

``` markdown
Based on all sources, what are the biggest contradictions you can find between the risks listed in the 10-K and the optimistic tone of the earnings call?
```

``` markdown
Synthesize the leadership's main strategic priorities as mentioned in the earnings call, the 10-K, and the CEO's YouTube interview.
```


``` markdown
Create a list of all the members of the Board of Directors and note any concerns raised about them in the proxy statement.
```


## :material-numeric-5-circle:Synthesize and Summarize

The Studio is where you bring everything together. It helps you create powerful summaries that collate information from all your documents.

1.  At the top of the **Studio**, you will see suggestions for generating audio and video overviews, mind map and reports like an **FAQ**, a **Briefing Doc**, or a **Study Guide**. Click one to create a comprehensive, AI-generated summary of your sources.
2.  You can also create custom summaries. For example, You can choose language, length, what topics and sources AI hosts should focus on via customize option. 
3.  Once the overview is generated, you can access it in the **Studio**. This allows you to build a custom dashboard of insights, combining automatically generated summaries with your own specific queries.


## Conclusion: Your Project Workspace

As you've seen, working in NotebookLM is fundamentally different from a one-off chat with Gemini. NotebookLM is a **project workspace** centered around a specific set of source documents. The **Chat** feature is your tool for ad-hoc querying and exploring specific lines of inquiry. The **Studio** is where you synthesize your findings, saving key answers and generating powerful overviews that bring together all the information from your source documents.
