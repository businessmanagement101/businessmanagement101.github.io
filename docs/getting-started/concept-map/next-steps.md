# Conclusion & Next Steps

## **Resources & Next Steps** 🚀

You now have all the tools and practice you need to begin working on your **Concept Map & Analytical Narrative** assignment. This page provides a few recommended tools, a quick guide to what makes a map effective, and a checklist to get you started.

### **Software Recommendations**

While you can draw a concept map by hand, using software makes it much easier to edit and organize. Here are a few recommendations:

* **Simple & Free:** [Miro](https://miro.com/) (free plan), [Coggle](https://coggle.it/), or even Google Drawings/Slides are excellent and easy-to-use options.
* **More Advanced:** [Lucidchart](https://www.lucidchart.com/) offers more powerful features for complex diagrams.



### **What Makes a Great Map? (A Quick Guide)**

As you build your map, keep these principles in mind. Your goal is to create an analytical tool, not just a list of words.

| A Strong Concept Map...                                    | A Weak Concept Map...                                        |
| :--------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Is **hierarchical**, with the main idea at the top.        | Is a messy "spider web" with no clear structure.             |
| Uses clear **linking words** on every single line.         | Has lines with no labels, leaving the relationships unclear. |
| Has insightful **cross-links** between different branches. | Is just a collection of separate lists in bubbles.           |
| Is easy to read and logically organized.                   | Looks more like a flowchart (showing a process) or a mind map (showing only one central topic). |
